HOW IT WORKS

Configure it here. Run it on your computer.

Build A Trading Bot does not run your trading bot in the cloud. Use the builder below to choose your strategy, rules and controls. When you're finished, download the bot package, install it on your Windows PC and run it locally.

1

Configure

Choose your bot's strategy, entry and exit rules, symbols, risk controls and other available settings.

2

Build

Your selections are turned into a configured bot package using the options you selected.

3

Download & Install

Download the finished Windows package and install the bot on your own computer.

4

Run Locally

The installed bot runs on your PC. Broker connections, credentials and trading execution are configured locally — not entered into the public bot builder.
