WHY AUTOMATE?

Why Build a Trading Bot?

A trading bot turns your strategy into a defined set of rules that can monitor the market and act consistently — without requiring you to sit in front of a chart all day.

Trade the Rules, Not the Emotion

Define your entry, exit and risk rules before the trade happens. Your bot follows those instructions consistently instead of hesitating, chasing a move or changing the plan during a trade.

Stop Staring at Charts

Let the bot monitor the market while you do something else. When the conditions you've defined occur, the bot can recognize them automatically.

React Immediately

Automation can respond when your trading conditions are met without waiting for you to notice the signal, open an order ticket and manually enter the trade.

Build In Risk Controls

Position size, initial stops, trailing stops and exit conditions can be defined before a position is opened rather than decided after the market starts moving.

Make Strategies Repeatable

Turn a trading idea into specific rules that can be executed the same way each time. That makes a strategy easier to test, review and improve.

You Stay in Control

Choose the symbol, direction, indicators, position size, entry rules, exit rules and risk controls. Start in paper mode and decide when — or whether — live execution should be enabled.
