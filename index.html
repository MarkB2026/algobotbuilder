ABOUT SCRIPTSAI

Software ideas turned into working tools.

ScriptsAI is a home for practical software created through human direction and AI-assisted development. The goal is simple: build useful tools, keep them understandable, and make them easy to run.

The applications shown here are not autonomous AI trading systems. AI is part of the development process; the software itself does exactly what it says it does.

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

Your financial data does not need another cloud.

ScriptsAI desktop tools are designed to run locally. Broker connections are used only for the specific read-only information a tool needs. Trading data and application databases remain on your computer unless you choose to export or share them.
