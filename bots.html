PLANNED SHARED CAPABILITY

HAL-assisted autonomy — optional on upcoming bots

Each bot keeps its own strategy and local execution controls. When supported, users can enable HAL to analyze candidates, manage entries and exits, tighten stops, and override strategy signals. HAL cannot override account-level safety limits, position caps, daily loss limits or the emergency stop. Paper/observe modes come first; integration and live permissions are subject to testing.
