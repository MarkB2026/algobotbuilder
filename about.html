Local first

Your bot. Your computer. Your rules.

Our downloadable applications are designed to run locally. You control the strategy and configuration. Optional connected features, such as broker APIs or BeeBot's planned Hive sharing, require their own setup and availability.

A practical note: Automated trading still involves financial risk, software failures, connectivity interruptions, slippage and rejected orders. A bot must be running and properly connected for unattended actions to work. Begin in WATCH or PAPER mode, understand the settings, and verify each broker integration before considering LIVE trading. Feature availability varies by bot and release.
